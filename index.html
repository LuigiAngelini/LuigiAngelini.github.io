About me

Welcome! I am a PhD student in Physics at Northeastern University (Boston, MA, USA), where I do research in theoretical high energy physics. I am interested in various aspects of string phenomenology, algebraic geometry, algebraic topology and number theory, as well as applications of machine learning to string theory and number theory.
