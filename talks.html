# Perturbations of KW Theory

MPLunch October 21, 2026

The holographic dual to type IIB string theory compactified on a Calabi–Yau cone with N D3-branes placed at its tip is an $\mathcal{N}=1$ $SU(N)\times SU(N)$ SYM theory, called Klebanov-Witten theory. In this talk, I give an introduction to Maldacena's holographic duality starting from $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang–Mills theory, I discuss its generalization to KW theory and finally discuss how perturbations of the Imaginary Self-Dual (ISD) supergravity solution in type IIB are described in the dual CFT.
